# UI/UX Feedback & Usability Improvements

Suggestions for improving the e-wallet app's usability based on best practices from leading apps like MoMo and ZaloPay.

---

### **1. Home Screen QR Scanner Widget**
- **Feedback:** Currently, users must open the app, wait for the splash screen, and find the scan button.
- **Suggestion:** Implement a home-screen widget or a 3D touch/long-press shortcut on the app icon to open the scanner directly.
- **Benefit:** Significantly speeds up the payment process in high-traffic retail environments.

### **2. Privacy: "Hide Balance" Toggle**
- **Feedback:** Financial privacy is compromised when using the app in public crowded places.
- **Suggestion:** Add an "eye" icon next to the wallet balance on the home screen to mask the amount ($****).
- **Benefit:** Protects sensitive financial information from "shoulder surfing."

### **3. Smart Amount Suggestions**
- **Feedback:** Typing amounts for frequent top-ups is tedious.
- **Suggestion:** Provide "Quick Amount" buttons (e.g., $10, $50, $100) based on the user's historical top-up patterns.
- **Benefit:** Minimizes typing effort and reduces the chance of input errors.

### **4. Transaction Status Color Coding**
- **Feedback:** The transaction history list can be hard to scan for specific results.
- **Suggestion:** Use distinct colors for transaction status: 🟢 **Green** for Success, 🔴 **Red** for Failed, 🟡 **Yellow** for Pending.
- **Benefit:** Allows users to visually filter their history at a glance for successful or failed attempts.
